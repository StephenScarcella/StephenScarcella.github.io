    Inbound: about me

    I run warehouse operations for a luxury jewelry and handbag brand at a 3PL in Newark, New Jersey. I started on the floor in October 2021 and worked my way up to operations manager.

    Along the way I learned to build software. Our warehouse management system, the label printing, the cycle count tools and the billing are all things I designed and built myself with Claude, and the floor uses them every day.

    Power Outage

    Browser game · TypeScript · built with Claude

    A grid-operator strategy game. You're the balancing authority for a real U.S. state's power grid, and your job is to keep generation equal to demand while managing cost and carbon.

    The scenarios are real events. In Texas you run ERCOT through Winter Storm Uri in February 2021: an islanded grid, gas wellheads freezing, prices pinned at the cap and demand climbing toward 77 GW. Plants you push too hard in the cold trip offline.
